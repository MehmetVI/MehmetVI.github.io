Privacy policy

Effective: 9 October 2026 · Rush Out for iOS, TestFlight and Android test versions, and this website

Rush Out keeps campaign progress on your device. Apple handles iOS payments and Game Center. Android test versions use Google Play for purchases and Play Games for identity; purchased Android balances are stored by our Unity services. Unity’s purchasing SDK and optional league services process information as explained below. Versions with advertising offer optional rewarded videos and infrequent interstitials between eligible completed levels; earlier ad-free versions keep advertising disabled.

Who to contact

For questions about Rush Out or requests concerning your information, contact the game’s developer, MehmetVI, at support.rushout@gmail.com.

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any diagnostics you choose to include to respond, investigate the issue or handle your privacy request. We keep this correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Game progress and settings

On iOS, the game stores progress, coins, owned vehicle appearances, boosters, settings and pending purchase identifiers locally on your device. It also keeps local competitive records and profile information needed for its Game Center features. These records let the game resume, preserve purchases and avoid granting a purchase twice. Device backups may contain local app data depending on your Apple settings.

A limited recent-event list is held in memory for game diagnostics. The current implementation does not send that event list to a separate analytics service operated by the developer.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center account information. Rush Out can use the identity and display name returned by Apple for your optional Game Center profile. Switch City keeps campaign progress and daily results on your device. If you explicitly join the optional seasonal league, eligible puzzle actions and verified results are also processed by Unity’s online league services, as described below. You can manage Game Center through your device’s settings. See Apple’s privacy information.

Player profile, public ID and chosen name

In versions with the home-screen profile, opening your profile can contact Unity Authentication, Cloud Code and Cloud Save to create or retrieve a stable player ID. A guest uses a separate anonymous service account; a linked player uses the authenticated account. “Anonymous” means that you do not need to provide a real name or email for that guest account; the service still keeps an account identifier and its public-ID binding. This profile request does not enroll you in a season or upload your campaign results. Your guest ID may not be recoverable after reinstalling the app. The public ID is a label you may copy and share, not a password.

A guest’s chosen game name is saved locally. If you use the linked career/league account, we store the chosen name, a normalized name reservation, change count, revision and pending/completed change-operation records on the service to enforce unique names and settle any in-game coin charge once. Linked names and league statistics may be visible to other participants. You do not need to use your real name. Changing a name does not remove earlier operation records needed to recover an interrupted change or prevent duplicate charges.

You can request deletion of a guest cloud identity from Profile → Guest account, or manage a linked account through its league/account controls. Guest deletion removes that guest cloud account and public-ID binding after completion is verified; local campaign progress and a separate linked account are preserved. A request can remain pending during processing. The app keeps a private receipt to check deletion status; its server-side verification record is valid for 30 days. Account data otherwise remains until deletion is completed. These controls do not delete records independently controlled by Apple, Google or Unity’s separate advertising or purchasing services. You can also contact support for an access, correction or deletion request.

Purchases

On iOS, Apple processes in-app payments. The game receives product and transaction identifiers and signed purchase records to deliver purchased items, restore eligible purchases and avoid granting the same purchase twice. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Rush Out uses Unity In-App Purchasing. In addition to handling purchases, this SDK sends purchase activity to Unity for purchase analytics and service operation. This can include product and transaction identifiers, prices and quantities, signed purchase records, platform identifiers such as IDFV on Apple devices and installation/session identifiers on Android, and technical information such as device model, operating system, language, locale and installation time. These SDK events are separate from the game’s local recent-event list. See Unity’s player privacy policy and Unity IAP privacy overview. Apple and Unity may retain purchase-related records after you remove the game.

Android identity, purchases and league

Android test versions use Google Play Games and Unity Authentication to identify an online account. We process the service player identifier, Play Games identifier and display name. The offline campaign does not require joining the league. Android purchases and spending purchased balances require an authenticated online account; game-earned coins and boosters remain local.

Google Play handles Android payments. Our Unity Cloud Code and Cloud Save services validate product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state and an account-linked hash with Google, then store purchased balances and transaction records. Device transaction identifiers and pending-operation records help recover interrupted purchases and spending without granting or charging the same in-game balance twice. Refunds adjust purchased balances. We do not receive payment card details. A pending transaction may temporarily require reconnecting to the same account before gameplay can continue safely.

Joining the optional league also sends puzzle actions and verified results to Unity services. Display names, cups and verified statistics are visible to other league participants; purchase tokens and authentication credentials are not public. Google and Unity also process service and purchase information under their own policies. Unity describes Android IAP collection in its Google Play data safety disclosure; this includes identifiers, purchase history and technical diagnostics, and may include transient approximate location and email processing depending on the purchase flow. The game does not request access to your contacts, messages or precise location.

You can request Android online-account deletion through the game’s account controls or our support page. Deletion removes the online account, purchased Coin and booster balances, league records and developer-controlled transaction records after pending operations are resolved and removal is verified. Purchased balances are not transferred to a new account. Locally earned campaign progress and resources are preserved. A private deletion-status receipt remains valid for 30 days. Deletion does not delete your Google account or purchase records independently retained by Google or Unity; see Google’s privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

Advertising availability depends on the version you use. Earlier ad-free releases keep advertising and advertising-consent flows disabled. Advertising-enabled test versions may use Unity LevelPlay to select ads from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads; other advertising-enabled versions use Google AdMob. The game selects one advertising provider for a build. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) also supplies privacy choices in LevelPlay versions, without the game starting Google’s ad-serving flow in those versions.

Advertising-enabled versions offer optional rewarded videos and infrequent interstitials at eligible transitions after winning a level. Interstitials do not appear during active play or in the first ten campaign levels. A permanent Remove Ads purchase disables interstitials; rewarded videos remain optional. There are no banner or app-open ads. Watching a rewarded video is your choice; the stated reward depends on the advertising service’s completion confirmation, not accepting a privacy choice or tapping an advertiser link.

UMP checks privacy requirements at startup and presents choices where required. In the current iOS LevelPlay test candidate, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency choice is presented separately after the UMP flow. LevelPlay initializes only when UMP permits ad requests and Apple tracking authorization has been granted. In advertising-enabled versions, the selected service can then load ads before you press a video button or see an ad. Where required, Settings provides an advertising privacy option to revisit your choices. Changing these choices clears loaded ads before further requests. Declining a choice does not block the campaign; ads may be limited or unavailable.

Depending on the active provider, advertising services process device and app identifiers, IP-derived general location, ad views and interactions, and technical information such as device model, language, operating system, network connection and performance. These support ad delivery, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. Unity’s advertising disclosures also describe purchase-related information and app usage information for applicable SDK features. Google’s ad-serving SDK describes crash and other diagnostics. Provider disclosures explain the scope of each service; using test ads or requesting restricted advertising does not mean that no information is processed.

The current iOS LevelPlay candidate requests Apple’s permission for device information to be used across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising and measurement. If permission is denied or restricted, it does not initialize LevelPlay and these ads remain unavailable; campaign play remains available. You can manage Apple’s permission in iOS Settings. The game does not pass a player identifier to LevelPlay initialization and sends restrictive regional-consent and do-not-sell/share signals before initialization. The separate AdMob-enabled iOS configuration does not request ATT, requests non-personalized treatment and disables Google’s publisher first-party ID. These settings restrict advertising treatment; they do not stop all service data processing. Android identifiers can include the advertising ID and app set ID when available. Rush Out does not add your public player ID, league name, email or purchase records as advertising-targeting parameters; this is separate from information processed by the SDKs themselves.

See Unity’s player privacy policy, LevelPlay’s data disclosure, Unity Ads’ data disclosure, Google’s iOS and Android data disclosures, and Google’s privacy policy. Advertising providers apply their own retention and privacy controls. Deleting a Rush Out guest or league account does not automatically delete advertising records independently controlled by Unity or Google. You can use the provider’s privacy controls or contact us for help directing your request.

This website

This static website does not use advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or its own cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses and other request information for security and operating the service. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Storage, deletion and your choices

Local game data generally remains until it is removed from the device or the game replaces it. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete backups, Apple purchase or Game Center records, or information already processed by Google or Unity. Those providers apply their own retention policies and offer their own account and privacy controls.

You can contact the developer about access, correction or deletion requests, including requests concerning Unity IAP data processed on the developer’s behalf. Include only the details needed to explain your request; do not send passwords or payment card information. Requests concerning data a provider controls independently may need to be handled directly by that provider. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

Children and international processing

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, please contact us. Apple, Google, Unity and GitHub may process information in countries other than where you live, under the terms and safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

Changes

This policy may change as the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the game.

Optional seasonal league

In iOS builds with the optional seasonal league enabled, Seasonal league enrollment requires you to explicitly join. Unity Authentication also supports the separate profile-ID features described above. Unity Cloud Code, Cloud Save and Leaderboards process the service player identifier, Game Center display name, puzzle actions, and verified results, ranks, seasonal rewards and cup records. Your display name, cups and verified puzzle statistics are visible to other participants. Signatures and access tokens are not shown in public profiles. You can use the offline campaign without joining. League account, profile, result, seasonal reward and cup records persist on the service until a deletion request is handled; leaving the app or deleting it does not itself delete those records. In your league profile → League Account, you can request withdrawal or account deletion and check Request Status. Withdrawal stops new uploads and hides your in-app profile; private statistics and reward records remain, and removal from the provider leaderboard may still be pending. Withdrawal is not account deletion. Account deletion is processed automatically on the server and can take several hours, including retries when services are unavailable. An accepted request remains pending until completion is verified; acceptance alone is not confirmation of deletion. The app can check a private deletion-status receipt after the original cloud sign-in has been removed. A hashed receipt is retained for status verification with a 30-day validity period. Local campaign progress, purchases and already earned rewards are preserved. Deleting the league account does not delete your Apple account or independently retained Apple or Unity purchase records. For help, contact support.rushout@gmail.com.
