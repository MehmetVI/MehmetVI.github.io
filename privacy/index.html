Privacy policy

Effective: 28 September 2026 · Rush Out for iOS and this website

Rush Out keeps your game progress on your device. Apple handles payments and Game Center. Unity’s purchasing SDK and optional league services process information as explained below. The current public release is ad-free.

Who to contact

For questions about Rush Out or requests concerning your information, contact the game’s developer, MehmetVI, at support.rushout@gmail.com.

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any diagnostics you choose to include to respond, investigate the issue or handle your privacy request. We keep this correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, coins, owned vehicle appearances, boosters, settings and pending purchase identifiers locally on your device. It also keeps local competitive records and profile information needed for its Game Center features. These records let the game resume, preserve purchases and avoid granting a purchase twice. Device backups may contain local app data depending on your Apple settings.

A limited recent-event list is held in memory for game diagnostics. The current implementation does not send that event list to a separate analytics service operated by the developer.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center account information. Rush Out can use the identity and display name returned by Apple for your optional Game Center profile. Switch City keeps campaign progress and daily results on your device. If you explicitly join the optional seasonal league, eligible puzzle actions and verified results are also processed by Unity’s online league services, as described below. You can manage Game Center through your device’s settings. See Apple’s privacy information.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The game receives product and transaction identifiers and signed purchase records to deliver purchased items, restore eligible purchases and avoid granting the same purchase twice. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Rush Out uses Unity In-App Purchasing. In addition to handling purchases, this SDK sends purchase activity to Unity for purchase analytics and service operation. This can include product and transaction identifiers, prices and quantities, signed purchase records, a device identifier known as IDFV, and technical information such as device model, operating system, language, locale and installation time. These SDK events are separate from the game’s local recent-event list. See Unity’s player privacy policy and Unity IAP privacy overview. Apple and Unity may retain purchase-related records after you remove the game.

Advertising and consent

The current public release is ad-free. It does not offer interstitial or rewarded ads, and Remove Ads is not offered for sale. Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform code may remain included in the app package, but the advertising and consent flows are disabled for this release. The app does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission.

Earlier test builds used Google test advertising identifiers. Test identifiers do not by themselves mean that no information is processed. When advertising services are enabled, Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information, depending on settings, consent, region and SDK behaviour. See Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy. Advertising in any future release will require the applicable consent choices and updated disclosures. Being ad-free does not remove the purchase and optional league data processing described in this policy.

This website

This static website does not use advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or its own cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses and other request information for security and operating the service. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Storage, deletion and your choices

Local game data generally remains until it is removed from the device or the game replaces it. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete backups, Apple purchase or Game Center records, or information already processed by Google or Unity. Those providers apply their own retention policies and offer their own account and privacy controls.

You can contact the developer about access, correction or deletion requests, including requests concerning Unity IAP data processed on the developer’s behalf. Include only the details needed to explain your request; do not send passwords or payment card information. Requests concerning data a provider controls independently may need to be handled directly by that provider. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

Children and international processing

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, please contact us. Apple, Google, Unity and GitHub may process information in countries other than where you live, under the terms and safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

Changes

This policy may change as the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the game.

Optional seasonal league

In builds with the optional seasonal league enabled, Apple Game Center identity is linked through Unity Authentication only after you explicitly join. Unity Cloud Code, Cloud Save and Leaderboards process the service player identifier, Game Center display name, puzzle actions, and verified results, ranks, seasonal rewards and cup records. Your display name, cups and verified puzzle statistics are visible to other participants. Signatures and access tokens are not shown in public profiles. You can use the offline campaign without joining. League account, profile, result, seasonal reward and cup records persist on the service until a deletion request is handled; leaving the app or deleting it does not itself delete those records. In your league profile → League Account, you can request withdrawal or account deletion and check Request Status. Withdrawal stops new uploads and hides your in-app profile; private statistics and reward records remain, and removal from the provider leaderboard may still be pending. Withdrawal is not account deletion. Account deletion is processed automatically on the server and can take several hours, including retries when services are unavailable. An accepted request remains pending until completion is verified; acceptance alone is not confirmation of deletion. The app can check a private deletion-status receipt after the original cloud sign-in has been removed. A hashed receipt is retained for status verification with a 30-day validity period. Local campaign progress, purchases and already earned rewards are preserved. Deleting the league account does not delete your Apple account or independently retained Apple or Unity purchase records.
