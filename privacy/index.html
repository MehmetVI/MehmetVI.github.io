Privacy policy

Effective: 26 September 2026 · Rush Out for iOS and this website

Rush Out keeps your game progress on your device. Apple services handle purchases and Game Center; Google’s advertising services may process data when their SDK is used. This page explains those different roles.

Who to contact

For questions about Rush Out or requests concerning your information, contact the game’s developer, MehmetVI, at support.rushout@gmail.com.

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any diagnostics you choose to include to respond, investigate the issue or handle your privacy request. We keep this correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, coins, owned vehicle appearances, boosters, settings and pending purchase identifiers locally on your device. It also keeps local competitive records and profile information needed for its Game Center features. These records let the game resume, preserve purchases and avoid granting a purchase twice. Device backups may contain local app data depending on your Apple settings.

A limited recent-event list is held in memory for game diagnostics. The current implementation does not send that event list to a separate analytics service operated by the developer.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity, display name, scores, leaderboard and achievement activity. Rush Out uses information returned by Apple to display your profile and competitive results, including friends-related views where available and permitted. Results submitted to Game Center may be visible to others according to Apple’s service and your settings. You can manage Game Center through your device’s settings. See Apple’s privacy information.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The game receives purchase information such as product and transaction identifiers to deliver and reconcile purchased items. Unity’s purchasing SDK may also process technical diagnostics and purchase instrumentation separately from the game’s local event list; see Unity’s player privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may retain purchase records even if you remove the game.

Advertising and consent

Rush Out integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. The current test build uses Google’s test advertising identifiers; the SDK’s presence should not be understood as a promise that no data is processed.

Depending on your settings, consent, region and the features in use, Google’s SDK may process IP addresses (which can be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information. Google describes uses including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

When privacy choices are required and available, you can revisit them with “Ad privacy options” in the game’s settings. You can also manage applicable tracking permissions in iOS settings. Available choices depend on your region and the state of the consent service. The current game does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. This does not mean that advertising SDKs process no data; actual identifier availability and processing depend on the operating system, SDK behaviour and applicable choices.

This website

This static website does not use advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or its own cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses and other request information for security and operating the service. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Storage, deletion and your choices

Local game data generally remains until it is removed from the device or the game replaces it. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete backups, Apple purchase or Game Center records, or information already processed by Google. Those providers apply their own retention policies and offer their own account and privacy controls.

You can contact the developer about access, correction or deletion requests relating to information within the developer’s control. Include only the details needed to explain your request; do not send passwords or payment card information. Requests about Apple, Google or GitHub records may need to be made directly to those providers. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

Children and international processing

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, please contact us. Apple, Google and GitHub may process information in countries other than where you live, under the terms and safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

Changes

This policy may change as the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the game.
